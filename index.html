Faculty Outreach Plan

Contact Green/Yellow faculty first (Sept–Oct). Red-tier only after SOP is polished.

Email Template
Subject: Prospective PhD applicant — [Your specific topic] × [their recent paper/project]

Dear Prof. [Last Name],

I am a Master's student at NYU working on [1–2 sentences on your ECG/EEG/VQ-VAE work]. I recently read your paper "[exact title, year]" and found that [specific connection to your work].

I am applying to your PhD program for Fall 2027 and would be interested in joining your group to explore [specific direction — e.g., discrete codebook representations of ECG signals for cross-patient transfer learning]. I believe my background in [signal processing / VQ-VAE / wearable sensing] could contribute to [their specific project or direction].

Would you be open to a brief 15-minute Zoom call to discuss whether there is mutual interest and if you plan to take new PhD students for Fall 2027? I have attached my CV.

Thank you for your time.
[Your name]

Outreach Rules

Do reach out when you can name a specific paper of theirs and explain a concrete research connection. Target 8–12 emails total.

Do not reach out with generic interest. If you cannot name a specific paper, wait until you can.

Follow-up: One follow-up after 10 days is acceptable. After that, apply anyway — silence is not rejection.

📊 AVS — Application Value Score
AVS = ResearchFit×0.40 + ProgramStrength×0.25 + Accessibility×0.20 + BudgetEfficiency×0.15
Research Fit (40%)How well the faculty and program align with ECG/EEG/EMG signal processing, VQ-VAE/RVQ representations, BCI, and affective computing
Program Strength (25%)Overall program reputation, funding, and placement outcomes
Accessibility (20%)Admissions competitiveness relative to your profile — higher score means more likely to admit
Budget Efficiency (15%)Fee waiver availability and application fee cost
≥ 75 Strong 70–74 Good < 70 Moderate
